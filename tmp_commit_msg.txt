This is a large reachable trap, not a one-off: 4502 fields in the live catalog
are a numbered variant of a sibling in their own dataset. A plan asked for "two
fields to relate" can satisfy that literally and produce nothing.

- _near_variant_ratio_fields flags the pair only when it forms the two operands
  of one / or -, in the same dataset. Two unrelated fields in a ratio is the
  relationship the pipeline wants, and a sibling pair merely co-occurring in a
  larger expression is not this defect.
- The draft gate returns NEAR_VARIANT_RATIO, repairable: the topology is fine,
  one operand is the wrong field.
- The candidate prompt lists near_variant_field_pairs by name. Restating the
  rule alone would not help -- the trap is invisible while both names look like
  two different fields.
